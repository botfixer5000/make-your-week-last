Which plan includes Grok Bot? Premium vs Super vs Ultra

The short answer: on The X Subscription, Grok Bot is included on Premium ($30 a month), Super ($100) and Ultra ($200). Plus ($8) and Free don't include it. The main difference between those three is the size of the weekly usage pool your bots share with Grok and Cursor. Prices are list prices as of October 2026, before tax.

Grok Bot is included on Premium, Super and Ultra, "for individuals and for team seats" (Cursor help: Plans and billing). Team seats cost the same per seat as the matching individual tier, each seat gets the same usage as that tier, and Plus isn't available for teams (docs).

What you're really choosing: the size of the pool

There's no separate Grok Bot plan or meter. Each paid seat gets one weekly pool shared across eligible Grok and Cursor surfaces "rather than split into separate product quotas" (usage limits). Your bots, the Grok apps and Cursor's Grok models all draw from it. Super and Ultra simply include larger pools.

Three things follow from that:

  • Heavy Cursor use shrinks your bot budget, and the other way round.
  • Third-party models in Cursor aren't in the pool. They're paid Additional Usage from the first use, on every tier (docs).
  • Past the pool, you pay as you go. Further usage comes from prepaid credit. You control top-ups, Auto Top-up and a monthly additional usage limit under Additional usage credits at grok.com/dashboard/billing (Cursor help).

Nobody outside xAI publishes the pool as a number of messages or tasks, and "usage rates vary by product." The multipliers (4x, 15x, 50x) compare tiers with each other. They don't tell you how many routines you can run.

Already on a Cursor plan or SuperGrok?

Existing Cursor, SuperGrok and X Premium plans continue unless you change them. Switching is optional (docs). Before you switch:

  • Legacy Cursor Pro, Pro+ and Ultra include Grok Bot with weekly usage, in that order from smallest to largest. Extra usage there is called On-demand usage, capped by the On-demand monthly limit (Cursor help).
  • SuperGrok, SuperGrok Plus, SuperGrok Heavy or X Premium+ can be linked to a legacy Cursor account to grant Grok Bot usage. A link and a Cursor plan don't add together: Grok Bot uses whichever grants more. A link is permanent and can't be moved to another Cursor account. SuperGrok Lite doesn't include Grok Bot.
  • Switching to The X Subscription cancels eligible old Cursor and Grok subscriptions on the accounts you combine, with a prorated refund for unused time. App Store and Google Play purchases have their own rules. A completed unification is permanent and can't be undone (docs: Grok Bot on The X Subscription).

Before you switch, check usage at cursor.com/dashboard. After, it's under Usage at grok.com/dashboard.

How to pick a tier

  1. Start on Premium unless you already know you're a heavy user. It's the cheapest tier with Grok Bot.
  2. Fix the leaks before you upgrade. Grok Bot's help suggests pausing routines you don't need, widening schedules and narrowing Slack triggers "before you raise a plan" (Cursor help: Routines). Frequent routines are the most common drain. The weekly usage guide walks through it.
  3. Measure for a week. Write down how much is used under Usage at the same time each day. If the week still runs out, you know by how much.
  4. Compare in dollars. If your plan price plus what you spent on top-ups is more than the next tier for a couple of months, moving up is probably cheaper. If it's less, stay and top up with a monthly limit set.

FAQ

Does X Premium include Grok Bot?

On The X Subscription, the paid individual plans include X Premium benefits when you connect your X account, and Grok Bot comes with Premium, Super and Ultra. Separately, an existing X Premium+ subscription can be linked to a legacy Cursor account for Grok Bot usage (Cursor help).

Is there a Grok Bot free trial?

Cursor's help describes a Grok Bot free trial that's a usage credit with a 7-day window, and it never turns into a paid plan. A large job can use the whole credit at once (Cursor help). App Store intro offers are different and do renew unless you cancel.

Can my Mac and iPhone use separate allowances?

No. Both apps share one usage bucket tied to your signed-in account (Cursor help).

Does a bigger plan make my routines faster?

The pricing page lists "priority model access at peak times" on Super and above (x.ai/pricing). It doesn't promise anything about routine speed, so don't upgrade for that alone.
